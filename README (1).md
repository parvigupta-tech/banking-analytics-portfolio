Project 3: Customer Segmentation & Campaign Analytics
Business Problem: Marketing spend isn't optimized. How to maximize ROI?
What I Did:

RFM Analysis (Recency, Frequency, Monetary value)
Segmented 8,000 customers into 8 actionable groups
Simulated campaign performance across segments
Calculated ROI by customer segment

Key Results:

Champions (16.5% of customers): 44.76% conversion rate
Loyal Customers (13.8%): 31.58% conversion rate
At-Risk (9.7%): 13.47% conversion rate - needs retention
Lost (9.2%): 2.48% conversion rate - low ROI

Campaign Impact:

Focus marketing budget on Champions & Loyal (high ROI)
Targeted win-back campaigns for At-Risk segment
Reduce spend on Lost segment (low efficiency)
Result: 27,181% campaign ROI through smart segmentation

Skills Demonstrated: RFM Analysis, Customer Analytics, Campaign Performance, Marketing Insights
